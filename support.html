Support

PharmaSNAP AI · Last updated 10 October 2026

This page is for help with the app, not for medical help. In an emergency call 000. For a poisoning or an overdose call the Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26. For a question about your own medicines, ask a pharmacist or a doctor.

Get help

Email support@lordly.com.au. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Which iPhone you have helps too. Please do not email photographs of prescriptions or test results unless we ask for them.

For a privacy question, or to ask what we hold about you, email privacy@lordly.com.au. The privacy policy lists what the app collects and which companies receive it.

Signing in

The app needs an account. You can use Sign in with Apple, or an email address and a password.

  • If you created an account with email, open the confirmation link we emailed you on the phone that has the app. The link opens the app. If the email did not arrive, the sign-in screen can send it again.
  • If you forgot your password, tap "Forgot password?" on the email sign-in screen. We email you a link to choose a new one. Open it on the phone that has the app.
  • If you first signed in with Apple, keep using "Continue with Apple". Apple's private relay address can differ from your usual email address, so signing in by email may not find the account you made with Apple.

Saved chats

Tap Saved chats on Home to search conversation titles, reopen your earlier questions and answers, continue a conversation or delete it. Reading saved text does not request a new AI answer. An internet connection and the same account are required. If a save fails, use the retry action before leaving the conversation. Chats from older versions that were never saved cannot be recovered.

Chat history excludes original photos, documents, video and recordings. You may need to attach a photo again for a new visual question. Saved chats do not mean every previous question will always be answered again without credits.

Video and voice

On the camera screen, choose Video, then tap Record and Stop. Clips are limited to 15 seconds. The app selects up to three still frames on your phone; review them before sending. The original movie and its soundtrack are not sent for an answer. For a short question without an image, use the microphone on Home. Voice recognition and new answers require a connection.

If a question appears correctly as text but the answer fails, that is not necessarily a microphone problem. Keep the question and try again when service is available. Include the build number and the error text when contacting support, without sending private health details.

AI allowance

Profile shows your remaining credits and refresh date. Photos, video frames, questions and cloud voice share the allowance. Complex requests can require more credits than remain. Free and trial allowances are one-time; Annual refreshes monthly and Weekly each Monday, at midnight UTC. Unused credits do not roll over. Saved answers remain readable when new AI work is paused. The terms explain current limits and service-wide caps.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile, tap Delete my account, read the warning, then confirm. This permanently deletes your sign-in, your saved medicines, your dose records, everything in My health, saved chats and the photographs you kept. It cannot be undone. The privacy policy says exactly what is removed and what is not.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first, using the steps below, or Apple keeps charging you.

If you cannot get into the app, email privacy@lordly.com.au from the address on the account and ask us to delete it.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store, so Apple manages them. We cannot cancel one for you.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap PharmaSNAP AI, then Cancel Subscription.

You can also get there from the app. Go to Profile and tap Manage subscription. It opens your subscriptions in the App Store, where you tap PharmaSNAP AI, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are decided by Apple: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you bought a subscription and the app does not show it, open Profile > Plans & restore purchases, then tap Restore purchases. You do not need to use up your free allowance first.

What the app is, and is not

PharmaSNAP AI is information, not medical advice, and it does not replace your pharmacist or doctor. The terms of use list what it does and what it will not do.
