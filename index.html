What you can do in the app

  • Ask, and keep askingAsk by text or voice, read the sources and keep the conversation going.
  • Snap a pack or a pageRead medicine photos, prescriptions and health documents, including readable printed doses and directions.
  • Answers that use what you savedUse relevant saved Cabinet medicines, allergies and Health details with your permission when asking questions.
  • Many concerns, one checkCheck several ingredient or dietary concerns together for a selected or custom product.
  • Compare nearby pricesSnap or type a pharmacy product to see nearby pharmacy listings and compare the online prices they show within your chosen distance.
  • Reminders and a dose calendarSave medicines and custom directions, set reminders and track doses in the calendar.
  • You stay in controlReopen chats, choose Voice off, report a problem in Profile, manage or restore your plan, and delete your account.

Medicine links can include supported same-ingredient findings and label warnings. They are not a complete check of every interaction. A first document reading may show only what is printed; ask a follow-up to connect relevant saved details.

Allergen check

Check medicine ingredients and allergens.

Choose several allergens or dietary concerns, such as gluten, lactose, gelatin or vegan ingredients, and get a result for each.

  • Gluten
  • Lactose
  • Gelatin
  • Vegan

Pick a suggested product or enter your own name. Answers need evidence for that exact product; missing ingredients do not prove absence or allergy suitability.

Price compare

Check availability and compare prices at nearby pharmacies.

Find nearby pharmacies in Australia.

  • Lowest online price found
  • Your chosen distance
  • Sponsors labelled

For eligible non-prescription medicines and supplements, look for pharmacy website listings and the online prices they show, with the lowest online price found within your chosen distance. Sponsored pharmacies are labelled. Pharmacies without online listings are not compared. Listings do not guarantee stock, and the price in store can differ. Ask the pharmacy about availability.

Reminders

Set reminders to take your medicine.

Choose your times and a calm voice, a cheeky voice or a chime.

  • Calm voice
  • Cheeky voice
  • Chime

Record a dose and see it in Cabinet and the calendar, or edit and remove a reminder. Built-in sounds work offline; a chime may play if a spoken recording is unavailable.

Cabinet

Save your medicines.
Keep track of doses.

Search generic and brand names, or add a custom medicine.

  • Strength and your directions
  • Dose records
  • Reminders

Save its strength and your own directions, record doses, and choose reminders. Edit or delete medicines when your list changes. Relevant saved medicines can help answer your questions.

Saved chats

Return to your saved chats.

Search and reopen earlier questions and answers.

  • Search
  • Reopen
  • Dated details

With your permission, recent user-written details about a clearly named person can help later conversations. Historical details keep their dates and stay separate from your own Health records. Reading saved text creates no new AI answer; saving and loading need a connection.

Health details

Keep your health details together.

Save allergies, reactions, conditions and dated readings.

  • Allergies
  • Conditions
  • Readings

With your permission, relevant Health details and current Cabinet medicines help answer medicine and health questions, including questions about a new medicine photo or a chosen saved health document. Keep your list current and say whose medicines you mean.

PharmaSNAP provides information, not medical advice. AI can make mistakes. Ask a pharmacist or doctor about a treatment decision.
