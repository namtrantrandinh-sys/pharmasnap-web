Check medicine ingredients and allergens.

Ask about ingredients using information from the pack or leaflet. Missing information does not mean an ingredient is absent or that a medicine is suitable for your allergy. Ask a pharmacist when the evidence is incomplete.

Check availability of pharmacy products and vitamins at nearby pharmacies.

Find nearby pharmacies in Australia. For eligible non-prescription medicines and supplements, look for pharmacy website listings. Listings do not guarantee stock. Ask the pharmacy about availability.

See the pharmacy finder

Set reminders to take your medicine.

Choose your times and a calm voice, a cheeky voice or a chime. Built-in reminder sounds work offline. A chime may play when a spoken recording is unavailable.

Save your medicines.
Keep track of doses.

Keep your medicine cabinet and dose records together, and choose which medicines have reminders.

Return to your saved chats.

Search chat titles and reopen earlier questions and answers. Reading saved text creates no new AI answer. Saving and loading need a connection.

Keep your health details together.

Save allergies, conditions and readings you choose to share, alongside your medicine information. Use these details when asking questions in the app.

PharmaSNAP provides information, not medical advice. AI can make mistakes. Ask a pharmacist or doctor about a treatment decision.
